Data notes: EPL match analysis

Source
- Football-Data.co.uk, English Premier League, seasons 2016/17 to 2025/26 (10 files).

Cleaning decisions
- Files read as Windows-1252 (Latin-1) text so team names don't break.
- Season label taken from the file name (for example E1617.csv = 2016/17).
- Dates converted using the UK locale (day first).
- Kept 22 source columns plus Season; dropped all others.
- Missing bookmaker odds: ___ rows (___% of matches). Rows kept, probabilities left blank.
- Missing Referee values: ___%.
- Team name fixes: none.

Calculated columns
- TotalGoals, GoalDiff, HomePoints, AwayPoints, HomeWin.
- Bookmaker probabilities: odds converted to 1/odds, then divided by their sum (the overround) so the three add up to 100%.
- Favourite, FavouriteWon, HomeProbBucket.

Validation checks
- Total matches: Expectation met
- Every season has 380 matches: Expectation met
- Result vs goals mismatches: Expectation met
- Negative stats: Expectation met

Limitations
- One league and one bookmaker (Bet365), so findings can't be generalised.
